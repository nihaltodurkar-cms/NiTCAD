NiTCAD Unit 68 - approximate (unsourced) material values
=========================================================

Owner ruling 2026-11-26: where no source in hand gives a value, use a general
approximate value. Each such value is cited with basis "approximate" in the
material registry (src/physics/materials.cpp); `nitcad materials <name>` shows it.
Replace a value here and in the registry when a source turns up.

Not approximate but worth noting:
- aln_dielectric electron_affinity_eV 1.885 eV is the midpoint of 1.83-1.94 eV (arXiv:2407.17360), basis "derived".
- sapphire gap, offset and electron mass are the amorphous Al2O3 values (Robertson 2004, Salmani), basis "secondary".
- tio2 has no conduction-band offset to Si (Robertson Table 2: 0 eV), so electrons barely see a barrier.
- tunnel_mass_evb = 0 everywhere means "use the electron mass" (basis "default").
